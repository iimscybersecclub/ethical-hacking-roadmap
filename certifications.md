# 📜 Recommended Certifications

Certifications help get your resume past HR filters and prove a baseline of knowledge. Here is the recommended path for beginners.

---

## 🟢 Foundation Level (Pick One)

If you have zero IT background, start here.

*   **[CompTIA Security+](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/security):** The gold standard for entry-level cybersecurity. It is entirely multiple-choice (theory) but highly respected by HR departments and required for many government jobs.
*   **[ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)](https://www.isc2.org/Certifications/CC):** A newer entry-level certification. It covers similar ground to Security+ and is currently offering free training and exams for new entrants.

---

## 🟡 Junior Hands-on Level (Highly Recommended)

Once you finish this roadmap, aim for one of these to prove you can actually *hack*, not just take multiple-choice tests.

*   **[eJPT (eLearnSecurity Junior Penetration Tester)](https://securityblue.team/why-ejpt/):** An excellent, fully hands-on, practical exam. You are given a VPN connection to a lab and must hack systems to answer questions. Highly respected for entry-level pentesting.
*   **[PNPT (Practical Network Penetration Tester)](https://certifications.tcm-sec.com/pnpt/):** Created by TCM Security. This is a 5-day practical exam where you must perform OSINT, hack into an Active Directory network, and write a professional pentest report. It is rapidly gaining industry respect as a top-tier practical certification.

---

## 🔴 Advanced Level (Future Goals)

Look into these after you have 1-2 years of experience.

*   **[OSCP (Offensive Security Certified Professional)](https://www.offensive-security.com/pwk-oscp/):** The holy grail of penetration testing certifications. It is brutal, requiring a 24-hour hands-on hacking exam followed by a 24-hour reporting period. *Do not attempt this until you are very comfortable with hacking.*
*   **[CompTIA CySA+ (Cybersecurity Analyst)](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/cybersecurity-analyst):** A great mid-level certification if you decide to go the defensive (Blue Team / SOC) route instead of penetration testing.
*   **[CISSP (Certified Information Systems Security Professional)](https://www.isc2.org/Certifications/CISSP):** A management-level certification requiring 5 years of proven industry experience. Keep this on your 5-year radar.

---

## 💡 Summary Strategy

**The Best ROI Path for Beginners:**
1. Finish this 6-Month Roadmap.
2. Study for and pass **CompTIA Security+** (to get past HR filters).
3. Study for and pass **eJPT** or **PNPT** (to prove practical skills in technical interviews).
